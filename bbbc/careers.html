What we offer

  • Compensation in bananas. Flasks, feasts, and repairs are covered by the guild bank for every settled raid night.
  • A full calendar. Two trading sessions per week, posted a month ahead, started on time. See the roadmap.
  • Professional development. Logs reviewed, strats shared, nobody yelled at in public. Value 02.
  • Equity. Every full-time trader gets a vote in loot council policy and a seat at the oath.
  • The dock. Off-nights, alt runs, mount farms, bad decisions in old content. The Company is somewhere to be.

What we expect

  • Attendance. Target 90 percent. Tell an officer ahead of time if you will miss; the desk plans around people, not surprises.
  • Preparation. Know the fight, have your gear enchanted, show up with your own bananas when the bank runs dry.
  • Temperament. We wipe. Everyone wipes. The question is what you type afterwards.
  • Not standing in the fire. Value 04. There is a ledger.
  • Neutral ground. Booty Bay is for everyone. So is the desk.

How to apply

STEP 1

Send your résumé

A link to your logs or armory, your main and spec, and your availability. Message an officer in game or post in the recruitment channel on Discord.

STEP 2

Interview

A short chat with the VP of Mergers & Acquisitions about who you are and what you want out of the desk. We are checking for temperament, not parses.

STEP 3

Trial period

Two to four weeks on the desk as an Intern. If it works for both sides, you take the oath on the crate and you are in.
