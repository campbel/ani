Mission statement

To deliver the finest bananas in Azeroth, at scale, on time, to every raid, every week, while generating sustainable long-term value for our shareholders, our employees, and the goblin who owns the dock.

The Booty Bay Banana Company exists for one reason: the continent needs bananas, and somebody has to be ruthless about it. We harvest them, we move them, we trade them, and when necessary we eat them in the twelve seconds before a pull. Everything else on this website is detail.

Vision

By the close of fiscal year 2030, the Booty Bay Banana Company will be the most trusted name in Azerothian fruit commodities, recognized on both continents and at least two islands, with a trading desk that clears every tier of content on the calendar and a balance sheet that could survive one more Cataclysm.

We will get there the way we always have: by showing up on Tuesday.

Our values

Every employee of the Company, from the Chief Executive down to the newest intern on the Harvest team, swears to five principles. The oath is administered on a crate of Stranglethorn Standard bananas in the Salty Sailor Tavern. It is legally meaningless and emotionally binding.

VALUE 01

Ripeness is a schedule, not a feeling

Raid starts at the posted time. Bananas do not wait for you, and neither does the desk. Be summoned, flasked, and inside before the first pull.

VALUE 02

Long on bananas, short on drama

We hold our positions and we hold our tongues. Disagreements go to an officer in a whisper, not to raid chat, and never to the Auction House.

VALUE 03

The dock is for everyone

Horde, Alliance, goblin, parrot. Booty Bay is neutral ground and so are we. Respect the people on the desk or find another dock.

VALUE 04

Do not stand in the fire

Risk management is a personal responsibility. Fire, void zones, swirlies, the bad side of the boat. Inventory lost to avoidable damage is written off against your name.

VALUE 05

Leave the dock better than you found it

Help the new hire, share the strat, put the feast down. The Company is the people in it, and the people are what we are actually trading.

Company history

The Company was founded on the lower docks of Booty Bay by a small group of adventurers who noticed two things: Stranglethorn Vale was full of bananas, and nobody was doing anything about it. A single crate, a borrowed cart, and a handshake with the Blackwater Raiders later, the Booty Bay Banana Company was open for business.

In the years since, the Company has grown from a handful of pickers into a fully staffed trading desk with harvest operations across the Vale, logistics coverage on both continents, and a Risk & Compliance function that has, on at least three occasions, prevented a wipe.

We have survived a Cataclysm, a Legion, a Shadowlands, and several reorganizations of the Auction House. We intend to survive whatever is next, largely by not thinking about it too hard.

A note from the Chief Executive

People ask me why a guild needs a mission statement. The honest answer is that it does not. But a company does, and the moment we decided to be a company, we decided to be the kind of company that takes its bananas seriously.

The joke is the point. We are a group of people who like each other enough to show up twice a week and pretend to run a commodities desk out of a pirate town. The paperwork is funny. The friendships are not. Both are real.

Thank you for your continued investment. Please see the FY2027 Annual Outlook for what we intend to do with it.

The Chief Executive Officer
